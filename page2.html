01 — THE ANCIENT BLOODLINE

The Origin of Sarkaz

Before they were called Sarkaz, they were known as Teekaz. Their history is a story of an ancient homeland, a lost civilization, and a people searching for a place to belong.

I

THE FIRST CIVILIZATION

The Age of Teekaz

Long before the rise of modern nations, the Teekaz inhabited Terra and lived in scattered tribes. Their civilization eventually grew around the region that would become known as Kazdel.

According to Sarkaz tradition, the Farchaser was a pivotal figure in their early history. Through his discoveries and leadership, he united tribes and established the foundations of their civilization.

II

THE FALL OF A HOMELAND

The Age of Exile

The arrival and expansion of other civilizations brought devastating conflicts to the Teekaz. Their homeland was lost, their communities were scattered, and the survivors entered an age of exile.

The name Sarkaz became associated with a people without a secure homeland. Across Terra, many were treated as outsiders and forced to endure prejudice, poverty, and repeated conflict.

III

THE STRUGGLE FOR KAZDEL

A Nation in Endless Conflict

For generations, the Sarkaz attempted to rebuild Kazdel, but their efforts were repeatedly threatened by war and internal divisions. Their painful history shaped how many viewed the outside world.

Their suffering did not mean every Sarkaz desired war. Beneath their collective history were people who wanted safety, dignity, and a future beyond the cycle of hostility.

IV

THE RISE OF THERESIA

A Different Kind of King

Following the death of King Yliš during the Eastern Crusade, Theresia took up the Black Crown and became the King of Sarkaz, also known as the Lord of Fiends. Her rise marked a turning point in Kazdel's history.

Theresia envisioned a future in which the Sarkaz could live with greater dignity rather than remain trapped in perpetual warfare. Her ideals offered another direction for her people.

V

REBUILDING THE FUTURE

Change Under Theresia

During Theresia's reign, Kazdel underwent efforts toward reconstruction. Economic activity, healthcare, and education received attention as the nation sought to recover from the devastation of war.

Theresia also established Babel, an organization dedicated to improving the lives of the Sarkaz and their treatment by the outside world. Through Babel, her vision extended beyond rebuilding a nation: it sought to change the conditions under which her people lived.

THE MEANING OF HER LEGACY

From Survival to Hope

Theresia could not erase the suffering of generations, nor could her ideals immediately end every conflict. Yet she demonstrated that the future of the Sarkaz did not have to be defined by their past alone.

Her reign and the creation of Babel became symbols of an alternative path for Kazdel: one built around reconstruction, dignity, and the possibility of change.

A people defined by their past can still dream of a different future.
